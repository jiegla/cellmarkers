# Terekhova et al. Immunity 2026 — immune aging reference

Source:
- Terekhova M et al. *Single-cell analyses of global cohorts outline determinants of immune aging and link the ratio of GZMK+ to GZMB+ Tem cells to health trajectories*.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.immuni.2026.09.012

## Scope

This folder stores a human PBMC immune-aging reference derived from the article.

The current `markers.csv` contains the initial curated marker reference for major populations and the key article-associated CD8 Tem GZMK+/GZMB+ populations.

## Important curation note

The article defines a harmonized immune population atlas. The supplementary material contains population annotations and canonical marker information. The complete population-by-population marker extraction from supplementary heatmaps/tables will be added as a subsequent revision after manual verification.

## Current priority populations

- CD8+ Tem GZMK+
- CD8+ Tem GZMB+
- CD8+ naive T cells
- NK populations
- B-cell populations
- Myeloid populations

`review_status=needs_review` indicates that the marker list requires final source-table verification, not that the biological annotation is incorrect.
